Privacy Policy for Catch It

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Catch It application on Android (package name com.majid.catch_it) and iOS (bundle identifier com.ramlisoft.catchit), developed by Majid Ramli (“we”).

Catch It is a utility that turns text or a link you choose — including a maps link — into a QR code on your screen, so you can show it to someone else to scan. You can also copy that text to your device clipboard.

Information we collect

Catch It does not collect, store, process, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information. No user account is required. No personal data is sent to the developer or to any server operated by us.

Any text you type, paste, or share into the app is processed locally on your device only, to generate a QR code or to copy that text when you ask the app to do so.

The app may store a simple local flag on your device so the short tutorial video is not shown automatically again after the first launch. That flag is not personal information and never leaves your device.

Data sharing

Catch It does not automatically share any information with third parties. If you use your device’s share features, or show a QR code on your screen, only the content you choose is shared, and that action is under your control.

Data storage

Catch It does not operate backend servers and does not store your data remotely. Text, QR codes, and any screenshots you take remain on your device.

Third-party services

Catch It does not use analytics services, advertising networks, tracking technologies, user profiling, or external data-collection services. The app does not use the advertising identifier.

Children’s privacy

Catch It does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from users of any age.

Security

Because Catch It does not collect or transmit your personal data, no personal information is stored on systems controlled by the developer.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised date.
