# catch_it

About Catch It App:

Share with QRcode

App Title: Catch It - Private QR Share
Subtitle: Share locations, not your phone number.

Description:
Stop sharing your personal contact information just to give someone a destination. Catch It is the ultimate privacy-focused utility that converts any location or text into an instant QR code for physical scanning.

WHY USE CATCH IT?
Have you ever been in a taxi or a ride-share and needed to share your exact destination, but didn't want to give the driver your WhatsApp, Telegram, or phone number?

Catch It bridges the gap. It "catches" the link from Google Maps and turns it into a QR code on your screen. The driver simply scans it with their camera or Google Lens, and the navigation opens on their phone instantly. No messages sent, no numbers exchanged, no privacy compromised.
HOW IT WORKS:

    Open Google Maps and find your destination.

    Tap Share and select the "Catch It" icon.

    Catch It instantly generates a QR code.

    Show your screen to the driver/friend to scan.
    Done! You’re on your way.

KEY FEATURES:

    Instant Share: Seamless integration with the Android "Share" menu.

    Privacy First: No phone numbers, emails, or social media accounts required.

    Versatile: While optimized for Google Maps, you can paste any text or link into the "Catch It" bar to generate a QR code on the fly.

    Lightweight & Fast: Minimalist design for high performance when you’re on the move.

    Offline Ready: QR codes are generated locally on your device—no data plan needed to create the code.

THE "CATCH IT" MISSION:

In an era where digital privacy is shrinking, Catch It gives you the power to share information in the physical world without leaving a digital footprint. Keep your contact info yours. Just Catch It and show it.
